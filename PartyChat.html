About Party Chat

Party Chat is a browser-based public chat room designed to let people join without an account or app installation.

Your browser connects to the existing encrypted WebSocket relay. Text, replies, reactions, presence, polls, countdowns, whiteboard events, images and GIF/sticker IDs are sent through that same connection to other people in the room.

Images are prepared locally at the highest practical quality that fits the WebSocket frame limit. GIFs and stickers are downloaded from the shared Party Chat library on GitHub with one click, then stored locally for offline use; only a small asset ID is sent through the room, not the GIF file itself.

Your chosen name and local chat history are stored only in this browser.
