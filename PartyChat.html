About Party Chat

Party Chat is a browser-based public chat room designed to let people join without an account or app installation.

Your browser connects to the existing encrypted WebSocket relay. Text, replies, reactions, presence, polls, countdowns, whiteboard events and images are sent through that same connection to other people in the room.

Images are prepared locally at the highest practical quality that fits the WebSocket frame limit. They are not uploaded to an image-hosting service, and the encoded data is never shown as chat text.

Your chosen name and local chat history are stored only in this browser.
